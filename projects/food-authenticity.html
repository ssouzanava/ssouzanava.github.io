Non-Targeted LC-MS Workflows for Food Authenticity

My PhD research focuses on benchmarking automated data-processing pipelines and chemometric strategies for non-targeted analysis, with the goal of supporting robust authenticity biomarker discovery in complex food matrices.

Research focus

Wine is used as a model system to investigate how analytical data processing and downstream chemometric or machine-learning approaches can be assessed for food-authenticity applications.

Methods and tools

The work brings together LC-HRMS data, automated processing workflows, chemometrics, machine learning, and biomarker discovery. Technologies include Python, R, LC-QTOF-MS, and Orbitrap.
