Small-Molecule Metabolomics for Food Authenticity

My PhD research applies untargeted LC-HRMS small-molecule profiling to identify phenolic biomarkers in wine. I develop reproducible Python pipelines and benchmark metabolomics data-processing software across high-resolution mass spectrometry platforms.

Research focus

The project benchmarks five metabolomics data-processing tools using data from Agilent QTOF and Thermo Orbitrap platforms. Wine provides a model system for profiling small molecules and identifying phenolic biomarkers relevant to food authenticity.

Methods and tools

The five tools benchmarked are MZmine, MS-DIAL, XCMS, Compound Discoverer, and MassHunter. Reproducible Python workflows support feature detection, peak alignment, adduct annotation, missing-value handling, and QC-based filtering. Statistical and multivariate analysis—including QC metrics, PCA, and PLS-DA—supports data-quality assessment and biomarker discovery. The work also draws on MS/MS fragmentation and compound annotation.
