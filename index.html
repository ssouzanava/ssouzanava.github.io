The Hybrid Scientist Advantage

Bridging Wet-Lab Experimental Reality with Dry-Lab Computational Precision

In computational life sciences, models are only as reliable as the experimental data that feeds them. My career began at the bench—designing molecular diagnostic workflows, PCR assays, and sequencing protocols—and evolved into high-resolution LC-QTOF-MS and Orbitrap mass spectrometry.

Today, as a PhD Researcher at BOKU University and FFoQSI in Vienna, I combine deep domain understanding of analytical noise, matrix effects, and biological variability with chemometrics, biostatistics, automated pipelines, and machine learning. Whether building predictive clinical classifiers or standardizing non-targeted metabolomics workflows, I engineer solutions that work in both code and reality.

Wet-Lab Roots

NGS, PCR, molecular diagnostics, and LC-QTOF-MS / Orbitrap instrumentation.

Automated Pipelines
